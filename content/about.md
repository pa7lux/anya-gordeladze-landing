Anya Gordeladze is a harpist, vocalist, musicologist, and founder of the Georgian polyphonic ensemble Giorgobistve. Over the past eight years, she has been studying the traditional and sacred music of Sakartvelo's regional traditions, learning from masters of Gurian polyphonic singing. Her artistry brings together Irish harp, voice, and diverse musical traditions from around the world.
